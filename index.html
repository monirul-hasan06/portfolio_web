About me

Learning by building real products.

I'm a Computer Science and Engineering student based in Mirpur, Dhaka. My work focuses on practical web applications, clear interfaces, authentication flows, data-driven dashboards, and experiences that feel solid on both mobile and desktop.

I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept to a working product. Recent work includes private messaging apps, storefronts, digital product delivery, community tools, and small browser games built for real use.

My current direction is full-stack JavaScript and TypeScript. I'm improving how I structure applications, validate data, manage authentication, and design interfaces that feel confident and consistent across devices.
